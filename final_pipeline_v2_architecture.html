Shared · Stage 1no ML · pandas only
Population Split
INfull snippet table (797,770 snippets / 588,832 unique reviews, verified against source) DODedupe to review level (rating/date/version are review-level, duplicated across snippets). Parse date → Indian FY quarter (Apr–Mar). Split into three strict, non-overlapping rating bands — Negative (1–2★), Neutral (3★), Positive (4–5★) — across every quarter in the dataset. Ratings are whole integers (verified: no fractional values), so every review counts in exactly one band — never split, never duplicated. OUTreviews{Negative, Neutral, Positive} × {every quarter, 2020–2026} — full corpus retained; which cohort pairs get compared — there can be more than one, e.g. the primary diagnostic (Q4 FY26/Q1 FY27) and the separate slide-8 deep-dive pair — is a downstream selection (Stage 7/9), not a Stage 1 filter
◆ CHECK: does each chosen cohort pair’s population clear the sample floor in both cohorts?  →  FAIL → report rate descriptively, skip the significance test for that pair · run separately for the primary pair and the slide-8 pair · this is a coarse pre-check — Stage 7 applies a finer, per-category floor later
◆ Independent by design — all three discovery passes below run in parallel and do not depend on each other. Each is its own bounded population, its own clustering fit, its own auto-labeled category set. Negative doesn’t need Positive to exist first, Neutral doesn’t need either — "what people are unhappy about," "what's mixed," and "what people love" are three separate questions, each answered on its own terms.

Stage 6cosine similarity, no clustering, version-blind
Generalized Measurement
INcategory_definitions_negative (the six/seven pre-registered categories — deliberately Negative’s only, see scope note) + every review, every quarter, all three bands DOEmbed each review’s text with the same model throughout, measure cosine similarity to each Negative category’s centroid, assign above a threshold. No clustering runs here — it’s a lookup against fixed definitions. Version-blind: every review is measured the same way regardless of app_version; that column just rides along on the output row for Stage 8 to use later. This is what makes "compare any two quarters" possible without re-running discovery, and lets a marketplace-era (pre-1P) baseline quarter join a comparison as just another measured population. OUTreview × category boolean/similarity table, every quarter, every band, for the Negative category set — the one table Stages 7–10 all read from
Why only Negative’s categories: these are the only ones with pre-registered hypotheses and P&L mapping that need tracking across every band and quarter (e.g. "does wastage linger even among happy reviewers"). Neutral’s and Positive’s own discovered categories (Stage 4″, Stage 4′) are standalone descriptive output — they go straight into the deck as their own "what people are saying" content, and don’t feed back into this measurement or into Stage 7’s testing.
Operating-model caveat (relevant to the slide-8 Before cohort specifically, see Quarters box): categories discovered post-1P may not transfer cleanly across the marketplace → 1P boundary (Sept 2025) — a low match rate on an older quarter may mean vocabulary shifted, not that complaints were rare then.

Runtime: this stage touches ~745K of the 797K total snippets (everything outside the Negative discovery window). Measured embedding rate: ~32 min alone on CPU — this is the single largest chunk. Negative discovery is empirically confirmed at ~3.5 min (see Stage 3a). Neutral and Positive discovery haven’t been run yet — budget roughly 3–8 min each pending their own resolution searches, same bounded-window approach as Negative. Total pipeline, revised for three discovery passes: roughly 45–65 min — likely comfortable under Colab free tier’s session ceiling, but chunked Drive-checkpointing stays load-bearing until Neutral/Positive’s real timing is confirmed.
